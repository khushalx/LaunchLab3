# LaunchLab

**A browser-based startup simulation game where every decision shapes your company’s future.**

LaunchLab is a strategy simulation game where you play as a startup founder and try to build, manage, and grow a company without running out of cash. The game is built around interconnected systems like product development, pricing, hiring, market strategy, reputation, runway, and user growth.

The goal is simple:

> Make smart decisions, survive the chaos, and build a startup that actually lasts.

---

## Overview

LaunchLab is not a simple clicker game. Every choice affects multiple parts of the company.

For example:

- Hiring a bigger team improves execution but increases burn rate.
- Lowering prices can increase adoption but reduce revenue.
- Shipping features quickly can increase growth but create technical debt.
- Ignoring product quality can hurt retention and reputation later.
- Strong market positioning can improve growth efficiency.

The game is designed to make players think through trade-offs instead of just choosing the obvious “best” option.

---

## Features

### Startup Simulation

Run your company week by week through scenario-based decisions. Each week presents a new situation, forcing you to choose between growth, stability, product quality, revenue, or survival.

### Product Portfolio

Create and manage products with different categories, pricing models, users, revenue, product quality, and growth behavior.

### Product Upgrades

Improve onboarding, fix bugs, add features, optimize performance, and reduce technical debt through a product roadmap and upgrade queue.

### Team Management

Hire different roles and manage the balance between execution speed, cost, morale, and efficiency.

### Market Strategy

Choose positioning, pricing, and market actions that affect adoption, competition, conversion, and long-term growth.

### Runway and Burn

Manage cash carefully. Burn rate, team size, product maintenance, and business decisions all affect how long your startup can survive.

### Timeline System

Track your startup journey through weekly reports, decisions, outcomes, and major events.

### Achievements

Unlock milestones based on growth, revenue, survival, product progress, team building, and strategic decisions.

### Save System

Progress is saved locally using browser storage, allowing players to continue runs and track previous progress.

---

## Gameplay Loop

1. Start with a startup idea.
2. Make weekly business decisions.
3. Manage product, team, market, and money.
4. Watch how decisions affect the company.
5. Survive, scale, or fail.
6. Try again with a better strategy.

---

## Tech Stack

LaunchLab is intentionally built with a simple stack:

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- LocalStorage

No frameworks.  
No backend.  
No paid APIs.

Everything runs directly in the browser.

## Why I Built This

I enjoy simulation and management games, especially games where decisions create long-term consequences.

LaunchLab started as an experiment to build a startup simulator where business systems feel connected. I wanted to create something that was more strategic than a basic clicker and more interactive than just reading startup advice.

This is one of my early serious development projects, and I’m improving it based on feedback.
